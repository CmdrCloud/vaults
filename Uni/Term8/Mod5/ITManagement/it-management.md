## Unit 1

The set of process, best practises, and tools that allow you to:
plan, deliver, operate, and improve services
that an organisation offers their clients.

It also consists of organising and managing resources to allow
users to receive information services in an efficient and secure manner.

### Hardware Management

- Consists in the installation and maintenance of devices
  (such as servers, computers, storage devices and network devices)

### Software Management

- Includes the installation, updates, configuration, and monitoring
  of OSs, applications, and information tools

> [!NOTE]
> such as updating arch through pacman

### User and permissions management

- consists in managing user accounts, access permissions, and every user
  must adhere to their employer's system access and usage laws and rules

### Cybersecurity

- area in systems engineering that aims to protect information systems and
  technologies against cyberattacks

- secure passwords

### Monitoring and Optimising Performance

- Monitoring performance allows feedback for developers to
  determine problems before the systems are used by people

- Similarly, optimising the performance of a system enlightens
  developers as to the problems within the system itself, and in
  the worst case scenario, the system itself could be flawed

- This is the worst case scenario, where the entire system must be
  either rebuilt or replaced by another system

- In the best case, the system would only need a significant
  performance boost that is not significant in effort enough to
  warrant an entire rework or replacing the system

### Backup Management

- Constant and reliable backup systems are an absolute non-negotiable
  necessity especially for information systems.

### Networking

- Networks are what allow communication between servers, computers,
  and other devices.

### Incident Management

- Managing problems that affect the functionality of technology services.

### Change management

- Change management evaluates the impact of a modification

### Constant Improvement and Planning

- Tracking changes and planning for the future as in the lifecycle of a
  project is a fundamental pillar of ITIL.

### What is ITSM useful for?

- Hardware management

## Unit 2

### ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library)

- A set of best practises for information technology.

- Its purpose is to IT services with a company's needs,
  standardising processes and improving quality, efficiency, and security
  in delivering services.

- ITIL allows companies to reduce risks, optimise costs,
  improve client satisfaction, and establish continuous improvement for themselves.

### ITIL Versions

- There are 5 main versions

- ITIL v3 introduces life-cycles into IT services
  - service strategy
  - design
  - transition
  - operation
  - continuous improvement

- ITIL v4 introduces a focus into the cloud and cloud-native services

- ITIL v5, announced in January 2026, introduces a focus on AI

| --------------- | --------------- |

## ITIL Life cycle

1. Service Strategy
2. Service Design
3. Service Transition
4. Service Operation
5. Continual Service Improvement

### What does that mean?

- service strategy
  - defines the vision and strategy for IT services

- service design
  - governs the design of IT services, including infrastructure, processes,
  and necessary tools

- service transition
  - implements IT services efficiently and with the least amount of impact on users

- operation
  - manages IT services daily, ensuring their availability and quality

- continuous service improvement
  - monitors and implements constant improvements for IT services

#### Service Strategy

- objective
  - design what services to offer and how to generate value
    - service portfolio management
    - financial management
    - demand management (anticipate future service demand, avoiding overloading)

#### Service Design

- objective
  - design new services or modify existing ones according to the business's needs
    - service level management (define standards for the service to comply with)
    - capacity management
    - availability management
    - cybersecurity management
    - IT service continuity management
    - service catalogue management

#### Service Transition

- Objective
  - build, test, and implement service in a production environment, minimising risks
    - change management
    - CMDB (configuration and service actives management)
    - version control and deployment management
    - service testing and validation

#### Service Operation

- Objective
  - ensure that services work effectively and efficiently everyday
    - incident management (restore services)
    - problem management (identify and correct root causes of incidents)
    - service request
    - event management (monitor and respond to events to detect problems)
    - access control and management

#### CSI

- Objective
  - continuously evaluate and improve services and processes
    - continuous service improvement

### Service Value System (SVS)

SVS describes how every component and activity in an org works together to create
value for clients, users, and other mutually interested areas

**SVS Objective:**

- Transform opportunities and client demands into value.
  - ...through management and efficient product delivery and services

#### SVS Components

The flow goes like this (top to bottom):

- Opportunity / Demand

- rectoral principles
  - fundamental recommendations that guide decisions and actions

- governance
  - norms and politics that direct and control an organisation

- service value cadence
  - set of key activities that allow you to create value for the client

- practises
  - practises that help you realise the necessary work to create value

- constant improvement
  - recurring activity to improve efficiency and efficacy

- Value

### 4 Dimensions

1. Organisation and People
2. Information and Technology
3. Associates and Providers
4. Value Flows and Processes

### Outsourcing

Outsourcing consists in hiring an external provider to totally or partially manage
IT services.

- Total (entire IT department)
- Partial (infrastructure, technical support, etc.)
- Offshore (in another country)
- Onsite (on the client's physical installations)

#### Disadvantages of Outsourcing

- Loss of control
- Security risks
- Communication problems
- Provider dependency
- Variable service quality
- Loss of internal knowledge

### Characteristics and Certifications

**Characteristics:**

- Contracts based on SLA (Service Level Agreement)
- Client-provider relationship management
- Risk control and complying with rules

Relevant certifications:

- ISO/IEC 20000
- ITIL Foundation
- COBIT and ISO 27001

### IT Administration

Planning, implementing, operating, and controlling tech resources in an
organisation to ensure that they support business objectives in an
efficient, secure, and sustainable manner.

### Planning Delivery, Operation, and IT Service Management Processes

These processes allow you to design and manage services that satisfy
client needs:

1. Planning: define what tech services the business needs
2. Delivery: implement changes or new systems with minimum risk
3. Operation: maintain daily functionality
4. Continuous Management: evaluate and constantly improve services

#### Planning

**Objective:**

Align services with the business's strategic goals, guaranteeing availability
and capacity for short and long term projects.

- service portfolio management
- demand management
- IT financial management
- capacity and availability management

#### Delivery

**Objective:**

Guarantee that changes and new implementations in services are made in a
secure and planned manner, while also trying to mitigate as much negative impact
as possible.

- change management
- configuration management (CMDB)
- liberation and deployment management

#### Operations

**Objective:**

Maintain services functioning adequately. Ensure quick attention to problems
and provide support to the end user.

- incident management
- problem management
- event management
- access management

#### Continuous Service Improvement

**Objective:**

Constantly review the performance of services and search for improvements.

- SLA review
- evaluate process maturity
- PDCA application
- user satisfaction surveys and KPI analysis
