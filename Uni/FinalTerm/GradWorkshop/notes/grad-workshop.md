## Scientific Research

- Latin "vestigare" (inquire, follow) which means "discover something"

- Systematic, controlled, critical, and feedback-centred procedure
  that aims to help the user discover or interpret phenomena/noumona, facts,
  relationships, or laws within a defined environment in nature

- Research is centred around searching facts when also never losing sight of
  your main objective

### Research Characteristics

- the search of something to pick up new knowledge that allows you to enrich
  the world of a science or discipline

- requires a strict verification and observation of the phenomena or noumona
  that is under study

- rigorous under a theory framework that encapsulates the whole problem and hypotheses

- transcends situations or edge cases in order to make a general inference
  towards validation

- uses a series of methodological instruments
