### Essential vs. Filling

For T2: essential concepts are things like domain concepts and the indicator
that your problem is modeled after (ETL, data storage, model), etc

For T5: concepts of the domain that the system measures or controls,
  a reference IoT architecture like ITU-T Y.2060, and filling is arduino history
  or generic definitions like what IoT stands for

### Fundamentals vs. Decorations

**Not like this:**

"cloud computing came up during the 2000s as an evolution of virtualisation..."

- this does not cite any personal decisions, it is a general summary

**Like this:**

"public cloud deployment is adopted because specific objective 2 requires scaling
  architecture without a personal infrastructure"

- that names the decision, source, and the objective that it comes from
- you know its written correctly if you change an objective - if
  you change the objective, you know that you'll have to change the
  paragraph as well

### Related works: what they are and what they are not

**OK:**

- previous studies related with my problem that "should not be confused with
  the history of the concept in question"

- reflects "the advances and the current state of the knowledge of a specific area"

**Not OK:**

- the list of apps or systems that are similar to the market
  without analysing them

- copying the context of another TFG without verifying that they are indeed similar

- a chapter from the history of what you're studying in your TFG
