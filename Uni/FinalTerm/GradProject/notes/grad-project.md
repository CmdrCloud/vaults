# Graduation Project Subject

- Trabajo Final de Grado: TFG
  - must be absolutely unique, or something that hasn't been solved

## What type of project will you do

- software development
  - requirements, use cases, architecture, data models

- business intelligence and data science
- networking, infrastructure, and cloud
- information security
- automation, IoT, and embedded systems
- IT management and digital transformations

## What you can/can't cite

**Yes:**

- known books
- official standards (ISO, etc.)
- IEEE, ACM, SciELO, Redalyc articles
- official technology documentation
- university repositories

**No:**

- Wikipedia
- Studocu, Scribd, Slideshare
- academia.edu, pdfcoffee, monografias.com
- personal blogs
- third party notes

## What is a problem situation (situacion problematica)

- when you have evidence that there is a real problem
