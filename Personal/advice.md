# Advice

- to manage change is to govern your fate

- a T engineer is someone who is deeply specialised in something (top 1%),
but also someone who can chat with other top 1% experts.

- To be a T engineer is to become one of the best in the world in every field.

- We should all strive to find a group of people who can govern changes in life.

## Why should we start with what we dream about?

- We don't plan without dreaming about where we want to be in life, so it is useful
to apply this practise to other physical things, like critical thinking or problem-solving.
